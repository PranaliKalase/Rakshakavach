# AI Governance & Safety Framework — RAKSHKAVACH

## Mandatory Principles
1. **Decision Support Only**: AI models generate review signals and priority scores. They never execute administrative actions or declare legal guilt.
2. **Standardized Terminology**:
   - Permitted: *"Requires Verification"*, *"Attention Required"*, *"Anomaly Detected"*, *"Verification Priority"*.
   - Strictly Forbidden: *"Fraud Detected"*, *"Guilty"*, *"Corruption Detected"*, *"Fraud Confirmed"*.
3. **Mandatory Model Transparency**: Every AI output discloses model version, feature version, timestamp, reason codes, and recommended actions.
4. **Mandatory Disclaimer**:
   > *"AI-generated assessment for verification prioritization. Confirm information against official records before making administrative decisions."*
